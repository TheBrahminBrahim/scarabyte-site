An indie studio that builds things worth using.

Scarabyte is a small, independent studio. No investors, no growth teams, no roadmap driven by ad revenue — just a studio that picks real problems and builds the best possible solution to them.

We think software should earn its place. Every app we ship has to solve something genuine and look great doing it. ScreenReps turns screen time into real movement your camera actually counts. PetFuel takes the guesswork out of feeding your pet a homemade diet. The problems are different; the bar is the same.

Our mascot is Baraka — a scarab beetle who takes the work seriously but not himself. He has a habit of turning up in unexpected corners of our apps and site, and reacting in ways best discovered on your own. Keep an eye out.

If it’s not useful and beautiful, it’s not done.
